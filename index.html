About

I'm a software developer based in Greencastle, Pennsylvania. I turn my ideas into projects people can actually use — from clean, responsive websites to tools that solve real, everyday problems.

I mostly work with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript on the web, and I've been digging into Python to build things like AI assistants and automation scripts. I like projects where I get to learn something new and ship something that actually works, not just sit in a folder.

When I'm not coding, I'm usually figuring out my next build or tinkering with an idea. If you've got a project in mind or just want to connect, feel free to reach out.
